KUBO Typeface - Crafted with love by Else Studio

## Introduction
KUBO is a monospaced geometrical display typeface, designed by Else Studio. Crafted using Bold Geometric shapes and in a Techno-Futuristic theme, this typeface perfectly captures the essence of modern innovation. Ideal for projects that require a cutting-edge, tech-savvy appeal, KUBO transforms an ordinary text into an extraordinary visual experience. Share KUBO with your mates and appreciate it to encourage us to put more of our stuff out! Create, Share, Collaborate & Have Fun!

## Font File
This download contains the font 'KUBO' in the following variant:
1. Regular - KUBO.otf

##  Installation
To install KUBO, follow these steps:
1. Download the KUBO.zip file and extract its contents.
2. Open the extracted folder and locate the font files.
3. Double-click the KUBO.otf and click the "Install" button.

## Usage
Feel free to use KUBO for personal and commercial projects – print or digital. This font is free, so go ahead and use it creatively and have fun.
